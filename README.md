# Create: WinterCOD Line

> A frozen world, a hungry lake, and one railway line between them — a survival Minecraft modpack built around **Create**.

## What is this?

**Create: WinterCOD Line** is a Minecraft modpack project set in an extreme cold world, built around the Create engineering experience — trains, stations and industry.

The name carries the three pillars of the pack:

- **Winter** — the world holds between **-20 °C and -80 °C**. Warmth, food and movement must be earned.
- **Cod** — fish from the frozen lakes are the only stable food source: humble, repeatable, essential.
- **Line** — the railway. Rails are the lifeline of the world, the only reliable way to move supplies and people between stations.

Where most Create packs treat trains as an endgame toy around a fixed base, WinterCOD Line makes the **journey itself the core loop**: planning, supplying and surviving long rail expeditions between outposts in a hostile climate.

## Design Pillars

- **On the rails** — progression is organized along the railway network, not around a single base.
- **Cold as a resource** — extreme temperature is a progression system, not just a nuisance.
- **Cod economy** — a deliberately humble food chain anchors the early and mid game.
- **Create-first engineering** — trains, logistics and industry are the main expression of player power.
